Four core modules behind Cubix's hospitality products

At Cubix Technology I design and build the Restaurant (F&B), Recipe, Inventory and Fixed Assets modules of Cubix PMS, the company's hotel management system. Cubix describes the PMS as having 16 purpose-built modules, 36+ connected OTA platforms and 100+ properties.

The same modules are reused in Cubix's standalone products: CuDine for restaurants, CuPOS for point of sale, StockMaster for inventory and CuFAM for fixed assets.

What I did

  • Owned each module across its full lifecycle: architecture, data modelling, development and cloud deployment.
  • Wrote the process flow diagrams, ER diagrams and SRS documents before development started.
  • Built the RESTful APIs that connect the modules to other platforms and the MySQL databases.
  • Managed version control, branching strategy and server deployments with Git.
  • Significantly reduced manual processing time for inventory and recipe workflows.
